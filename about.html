About Dr. Nouman Saleem Khan PT

Internationally Certified Physiotherapist & Chiropractor | Pain Management Expert

DPT (UOL) | Certified Orthopedic Manual Physical Therapist (OMPT) | Certified Cupping Therapist (UK) | Accredited Dry Needling Expert (UK)

Dr. Nouman Saleem Khan PT is the founder of Muhammad Advanced Physiotherapy, Bhalwal, delivering evidence-based care with international standards. He brings extensive clinical experience from leading hospitals including University of Lahore Teaching Hospital, Sir Ganga Ram Hospital, Lahore, and Saira Memorial Hospital, Lahore.

With advanced UK certifications from Prime Physio UK, Dr. Nouman integrates modern techniques with hands-on manual therapy. His core belief: "Treat the root cause, not just the symptoms" — ensuring long-term recovery instead of temporary relief.

His philosophy is simple: Every patient deserves treatment at international standards. That's why all protocols at the clinic follow globally recognized SOPs, with personalized care plans instead of one-size-fits-all treatment. Guaranteed pain relief from first session.

Core Specialties

Advanced Manual Therapy

OMPT-certified mobilization & manipulation for spine, joints, and soft tissue. Restores alignment, reduces stiffness, and improves function without surgery.

Chiropractic Care

International SOP-based spinal adjustments for disc issues, postural problems, and nerve compression. Gentle, precise, and diagnosis-driven.

Dry Needling

UK-accredited technique for releasing deep muscle knots, trigger points, and chronic pain. Effective for sciatica, migraine, and sports injuries. Trained under *Prime Physio UK* protocols.

Dry Cupping & Hijama

Certified Cupping Therapist (UK). Uses myofascial decompression to improve blood flow, reduce inflammation, and accelerate muscle recovery. Safe, sterile, and evidence-based.

Advanced Electrotherapy

Modern modalities: TENS, ultrasound, laser, and IFT for pain control, tissue healing, and nerve stimulation.

Clinical Expertise Across Disciplines

Advanced treatment for cervical neck pain, lower back pain, slipped disc, sciatica, frozen shoulder, knee osteoarthritis, and post-fracture stiffness. Specialized rehab for muscle weakness & muscle issues post fracture to restore strength and prevent re-injury.

Expert post-op rehab after orthopedic surgeries: ACL reconstruction, knee/hip replacement, spinal surgery, and fracture fixation. Focus on early mobility, scar tissue management, and safe return to daily activities.

Evidence-based rehab for stroke recovery, Parkinson’s disease, spinal cord injuries, Bell’s palsy, and balance disorders. Includes gait training, neuroplasticity exercises, and functional retraining to maximize independence.

Comprehensive care for ligament tears, muscle strains, tennis elbow, runner’s knee, and shoulder impingement. Sports-specific rehab with injury prevention and performance enhancement protocols.

Specialized care for seniors with osteoporosis, age-related joint pain, postural kyphosis, and fall risk. Gentle manual therapy and balance training to improve mobility and quality of life.

Early intervention for cerebral palsy, developmental delays, torticollis, flat feet, and postural defects in children. Play-based, child-friendly therapy to achieve motor milestones.
