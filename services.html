What We Offer

We offer a wide range of services to help you get back to your best. Our team of experts will work with you to create a personalized treatment plan that fits your needs. We are committed to providing you with the highest quality care in a comfortable and supportive environment.

What We Treat

Back Pain

Kamar ka dard, pathon ka khichao, ya disc ka masla, hum jarr se ilaaj karte hain.

Neck Pain

Gardn ka dard, chakkar aana, ya pathon mein khichao, hum aapko sukoon pohanchate hain.

Knee Pain

Ghutno ka dard, uthne baithne mein takleef, ya chalne mein pareshani, humara ilaaj aapke liye hai.

Shoulder Pain

Kandhe ka dard, frozen shoulder, ya kandhe ka utar jana, hum behtareen physiotherapy faraham karte hain.

Sciatica

Arqu-ul-nisa ka dard, jo kamar se tang tak jata hai, hum iska mukammal ilaaj karte hain.

Sport Injuries

Khel ke doran lagnay wali choton ka maaqool aur jald ilaaj, taake aap wapis maidan mein aa sakein.

Arthritis

Joron ka dard aur sujan, jise gathiya bhi kehte hain, iska jadeed physiotherapy se ilaaj karayein.

Disk Problems

Kamar ya gardan ki disc ka masla, jiska ilaaj hum baghair operation ke karte hain.

Frozen Shoulder

Kandha jam jana, jiski wajah se hath hilana mushkil ho, iska mukammal physiotherapy ilaaj.

Muscle Weakness

Pathon ki kamzori, jism mein kampan ya larzah, iska behtareen ilaaj physiotherapy hai.

Post-Surgery Rehab

Operation ke baad jism ko apni asli halat mein wapis laane ke liye physiotherapy bohot zaroori hai.

Rehab for Elderly

Barhay logon ke liye khas physiotherapy, taake woh apni zindagi sukoon se guzar sakein.

Spine Problems

Reerh ki haddi ke masail, jaise ke moro ka hilna, iska tashkhees ke sath ilaaj kiya jata hai.

Workstation Posture

Daftar mein galat tarah se baithne ki wajah se honay walay dard ka mukammal ilaaj aur hidayat.
